The problem

Institutions were running on paper records or disconnected tools, leading to duplicated work, delayed communication, inaccurate attendance, manual fee tracking, and slow report generation.

The approach

A single platform with role-based dashboards, so each role sees only what they need. Super Admin gets system-wide control, Administrator gets day-to-day operations, without either one wading through the other.

Two roles, two different needs

Super Admin

System-wide control: institution settings, user & role management, permission matrices, system settings, notification management, and audit logs.

Administrator

Day-to-day school operations: admissions, student and teacher management, class & timetable, attendance, examinations & results, and communication & notices.

Shipped

EduSphere's Super Admin and Administrator experiences are complete and functional end to end, from institution setup down to individual permission flows and empty states.
